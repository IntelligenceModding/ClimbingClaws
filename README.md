<p align="center">
  Climbing Claws adds a dedicated off-hand climbing tool to Minecraft for players who want controlled vertical movement without building ladders everywhere.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Equip the claws in your off-hand, hold right-click to raise them like a shield, and use them to climb walls and undersides. The mod stays focused on one traversal tool, but gives it enough progression and enchantment support to fit naturally into survival play.
</p>

<p align="center">
  Climbing Claws currently includes:
</p>

<p align="center">
  &bull; A dedicated <strong>Climbing Claws</strong> item<br>
  &bull; Off-hand, shield-style climbing by holding right-click<br>
  &bull; Wall and ceiling climbing on sturdy block surfaces<br>
  &bull; Hanging in place when you stop pressing movement while still holding the claws up<br>
  &bull; Slow controlled descent while sneaking on a wall or underside<br>
  &bull; Durability usage while climbing, with support for <strong>Unbreaking</strong> and <strong>Mending</strong><br>
  &bull; <strong>Efficiency</strong> support to improve climb speed<br>
  &bull; A light weapon profile with support for <strong>Sharpness</strong> and <strong>Fire Aspect</strong><br>
  &bull; Two custom enchantments for the claws:<br>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- <strong>Wall Spring</strong>: press jump while climbing to launch upward, with two levels and a cooldown<br>
  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;- <strong>Canopy Grip</strong>: lets the claws latch onto partial surfaces such as leaves<br>
  &bull; Custom enchant books available through normal progression such as librarian trades and loot chest finds<br>
  &bull; JEI ingredient info integration for the claws item
</p>
